Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a free trial? ▼
Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features in your chosen plan during the trial period.
Can I use PayPe ERP for GST filing? ▼
Yes. PayPe ERP supports GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and ITC reconciliation. The AI assistant can also help you find the correct GST rate for any product or service instantly.
How does the demo access work? ▼
Use the demo code PAYPE-DEMO-2026 to access our demo environment with 3 pre-configured logins (Admin, Manager, Employee). The demo has sample data and lets you explore all modules without any commitment.
Is my data safe? ▼
Yes. PayPe ERP uses bank-grade encryption, secure cloud hosting, and daily backups. Your data is stored in India-based servers and we are fully GDPR and Indian data protection law compliant.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▼
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, the change takes effect at your next billing date.
Do you support multiple companies? ▼
Yes. The Business and Enterprise plans support multiple companies. Each company has its own data, users, and settings — perfect for business groups and holding companies.
Is PayPe ERP available globally? ▼
Yes. While built for Indian businesses with GST, TDS, and Indian accounting standards, PayPe ERP works globally. Pricing is available in both INR and USD. The AI assistant supports multiple languages including English, Tamil, and Hindi.
